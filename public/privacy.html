Privacy

Vibez is a small listen-together radio run by bike-shed.io. This page explains what it does with your data.

Signing in with Google

Listening needs no account. If you sign in with Google to go live, DJ or add tracks, Vibez receives your email address, name and profile picture from Google. Vibez does not ask for any other Google data.

Vibez does not keep a user database. Your sign-in lives in a signed session cookie in your browser, or in a private file on your Mac for the Mac app, and expires after 30 days.

What other people see

  • Everyone in a channel sees the display name of every listener.
  • Everyone sees the DJ name, profile picture, room name and current track of live channels.
  • The owner of a channel sees the email addresses of signed-in listeners in that channel, so they can trust them as DJs.
  • When someone goes live, Vibez may post their DJ name and room name to a Slack channel.

What Vibez stores

Channels, listeners and queues exist only in server memory and disappear when a channel ends or the server restarts. The server keeps ordinary logs for troubleshooting. Vibez uses no analytics and no advertising.

Music

Tracks play directly from SoundCloud in your browser or app, so SoundCloud sees that request under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/bike-shed-io/vibez.
