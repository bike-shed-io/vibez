Vibez for Mac

Vibez lives in your menu bar: join a channel, listen together, and go live without keeping a browser tab open.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Install

With Homebrew, in Terminal:

brew tap bike-shed-io/vibez
brew install --cask vibez

Update

brew update
brew upgrade --cask vibez

First start

Click the Vibez icon in the menu bar and pick a channel. Listening needs no account; sign in with Google only to go live or add tracks. Links to a channel on this site open in the app via “Open in Vibez for Mac”.
